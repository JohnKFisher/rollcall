# Roll Call North Star

Roll Call exists to make youth sports feel special.

Game Day is the product. Everything else supports Game Day.

Priority:
1. Easy
2. Personal
3. Cool
4. Professional

Preparation is for decisions. Game Day is for execution.

Encourage. Do not pressure.
Reveal. Do not surprise.
Recover. Do not punish.

Users own their teams. Import/export is ownership.

Free should feel complete.
Premium should add delight and save time, not unlock the real app.

No ads.
No required accounts.
No social network.
No reliability paywalls.

Protect the product before protecting growth.

Codex should work item-by-item, surface conflicts, and wait for sign-off before implementation.
