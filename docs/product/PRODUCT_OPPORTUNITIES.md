Roll Call Product Opportunities

Last Updated: June 2026

Purpose

This document replaces the traditional feature roadmap.

Roll Call has reached a point where the core vision is largely complete. Rather than committing to a sequence of major releases, future development will focus on observing real-world usage, refining workflows, and experimenting with opportunities that may improve the experience for coaches, announcers, and teams.

Items in this document are ideas, areas of exploration, or long-term directions. Inclusion here does not imply commitment to implementation.

⸻

Current Product Position

Roll Call delivers the core experience envisioned at project inception:

* Team and roster management
* Player introductions with music and photos
* Apple Music integration
* Custom audio clips
* Reusable clips
* Game Day mode
* Readiness validation
* Team import/export and sharing
* Fully on-device operation

Future work should prioritize improving real-world use over adding features for their own sake.

⸻

Guiding Principles

Solve Real Problems

Preference should be given to improvements that emerge from actual field use, user feedback, or observed friction.

Keep Game Day Fast

The best Game Day experience minimizes navigation, configuration, and decision-making while a game is in progress.

Preserve Simplicity

New features should not significantly increase complexity for first-time users.

Protect Reliability

Reliability, predictability, and confidence during live events remain more important than feature count.

Active Areas of Interest

Live Presentation Experience

Ideas related to operating Roll Call during live games and events.

Potential opportunities:

* Pinned Game Day actions
* Quick-access favorites
* Between-inning music workflows
* Team entrance music
* Warm-up playlists
* Special event buttons
* Custom Game Day layouts
* Faster player navigation
* Expanded announcer workflows

Questions:

* What content do coaches use most during games?
* What requires too many taps?
* What actions need to be available instantly?

⸻

Multi-Sport Expansion

Opportunities to adapt Roll Call beyond baseball and softball.

Potential opportunities:

* Soccer support
* Basketball support
* Hockey support
* Sport-specific terminology
* Sport-specific event buttons
* Alternate Game Day workflows

Questions:

* Which sports naturally fit the existing model?
* What sport-specific workflows are required?

Team Identity

Opportunities to make teams feel more personalized.

* Team logos

* Team banners

Audio Enhancements

Potential opportunities:

Coach Convenience

Ideas focused on reducing setup and maintenance effort.

Potential opportunities:

* Batch editing
* Improved roster maintenance
* Additional import formats
* Faster player creation
* Enhanced sharing workflows
* Team duplication

Questions:

* What repetitive tasks consume the most time?
* Which setup activities create friction?

⸻

Experiments

The following ideas may be explored as prototypes, limited releases, or field tests.

Examples:

* AI-assisted clip suggestions
* Automatic song cue recommendations
* Team-generated event packs
* Community clip libraries
* Sport-specific starter templates
* Enhanced announcer tools

Experiments should remain low-risk and easily reversible.

⸻

Observation Log

Future priorities should be informed by:

* Real game usage
* User feedback
* Support requests
* App Store reviews
* Personal field experience
* Requests from coaches and organizations

Patterns observed repeatedly should drive future development priorities.

⸻

Success Criteria

Future development should be evaluated against the following questions:

1. Does this make Game Day easier?
2. Does this reduce setup effort?
3. Does this improve reliability?
4. Does this help teams create memorable moments?
5. Would a coach actually notice and appreciate the change?

If the answer is “no” to most of these questions, the idea likely belongs in the experiment category rather than the product roadmap.
