# Roll Call Product Scope

## Core Product

Decision: Game Day is the irreplaceable feature.
Reason: Roll Call is a performance app, not just a setup app.
Confidence: High

Decision: Team is the durable object; Game Day is temporary.
Reason: Games are mostly stateless.
Confidence: High

Decision: Preparation is for decisions; Game Day is for execution.
Reason: Users are under pressure during live use.
Confidence: High

## Free vs Premium

Decision: Launch 1.0 free.
Reason: Current premium candidates are not validated enough to gate.
Confidence: High

Decision: Multiple teams stay free.
Reason: Families and coaches naturally manage more than one team.
Confidence: High

Decision: Import/export stays free.
Reason: Ownership and backup are core trust features.
Confidence: High

Decision: Team identity stays free.
Reason: Making the team feel like their team is foundational.
Confidence: High

Decision: Premium should enhance delight and save time.
Priority: Creativity > Save Time > Pretty > Control.
Confidence: High

Decision: Premium must be visible before effort.
Reason: No sunk-cost paywalls.
Confidence: High

Decision: Existing premium content should continue working even if entitlement changes.
Reason: Users should not lose creative work.
Confidence: High

## Business Rules

Decision: No ads.
Confidence: High

Decision: No required accounts.
Confidence: High

Decision: No social network.
Confidence: High

Decision: Premium must never affect Game Day reliability.
Confidence: High
