# Roll Call Foundations

Status: historical summary

The original master plan and early project philosophy established the themes that still shape Roll Call:

- Game Day playback must be reliable under real field conditions.
- Setup should be simple enough for a coach or parent who is not technical.
- Apple Music convenience is valuable, but the app needs honest local and fallback paths.
- Team data should stay local, inspectable, recoverable, and exportable.
- Native platform behavior is preferred over hidden automation or speculative infrastructure.
- The product should favor clarity, reliability, and repeated daily use over feature count.

The early plan also assumed that the app would remain focused on one live job: configure a team, choose player cues, enter Game Day, and make the walk-up moment work. Those principles remain current in:
