What works now:
- Readable app-owned local audio and readable Music Library files can generate derived `.m4a` playback clips under `Application Support/RollCall/GeneratedClips`
- Generated clips bake the selected duration and fade envelope, retain original source/selection truth, and become the preferred playback recipe when ready
- Apple Music-linked songs without a readable local file stay source-backed; a library item that appears stored on-device can be marked ready here without being marked portable
- Preparation jobs are serialized, deduplicated per player, paused around live use and Low Power Mode, protected by generation keys, and retried no more than twice after the first failure
- Existing saved players and older `.rollcall` packages with legacy `cue` data decode into private player song assignments while preserving the same playback recipe
