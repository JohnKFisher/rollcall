This notice explains how the Roll Call project applies the root
[LICENSE](LICENSE), what is not covered by that license, and what is required
for public forks and modified builds.

This is a practical project notice, not legal advice.

## Summary

Roll Call is source-available for noncommercial use.

You may view, build, use, copy, modify, fork, and publicly share Roll Call for
personal, educational, nonprofit, public agency, volunteer, youth sports,
community sports, research, hobby, and similar noncommercial purposes.

Commercial use is not permitted under the public license. Commercial use,
including internal use by for-profit businesses, requires separate written
permission from John Kenneth Fisher.

## Public Forks And Modified Builds

Public noncommercial forks and modified builds are welcome when they follow
these requirements:

- Use a distinct app or product name.
- Do not present the fork as the original Roll Call app.
- Preserve source and documentation attribution to Roll Call and John Kenneth
  Fisher.
- Visibly credit `Derived from Roll Call by John Kenneth Fisher` in the app's
  About, Credits, Licenses, or equivalent screen.
- Link to the original Roll Call repository when practical.
- Publish the corresponding modified source code when publicly distributing a
  modified build.
- License that corresponding modified source under terms no more restrictive
  than Roll Call's public noncommercial terms.

Commercial use is not allowed unless separately licensed in writing by John
Kenneth Fisher.

- selling Roll Call, a fork, or a derivative app,

- using Roll Call or a derivative internally at a for-profit business.

If a use case is unclear, ask first.

Schools, nonprofits, public agencies, volunteer teams, youth sports programs,
and community sports organizations may use Roll Call for noncommercial purposes

If premium content, commercial restrictions, or other terms would get in the
way of a good community use, please contact John Kenneth Fisher. Free

Small, standalone code snippets from this repository may be reused commercially.
Attribution is appreciated but not required for those snippets.

This exception does not allow commercial reuse of Roll Call as an app, a fork,
a substantial portion of the codebase, the app architecture, UI flows, bundled
content, templates, branding, or derivative products.

The public software license does not grant reuse rights for all bundled
materials.

Reserved materials must be replaced in forks and modified builds unless John

- the Roll Call app name as a product name,

- sample personal or team media,
- any other personal or brand-identifying materials in the repository.

Forks may describe themselves as derived from Roll Call, but their app or
product name must be distinct.
